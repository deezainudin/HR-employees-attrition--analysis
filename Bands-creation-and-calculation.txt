
## 1. Bands (calculated columns on `FactEmployee`)

| Band column | Source column | Cut-offs |
|---|---|---|
| `Overtime Band` | `overtime_hours_per_week` | <5, 5-10, 10-15, 15+ |
| `Burnout Band` | `burnout_score` | <3, 3-5, 5-7, 7+ |
| `Engagement Band` | `engagement_score` | <4, 4-6, 6-8, 8+ |
| `Manager Support Band` | `manager_support_score` | <4, 4-6, 6-8, 8+ |
| `WLB Band` | `work_life_balance_score` | <4, 4-6, 6-8, 8+ |
| `Tenure Band` | `years_at_company` | <1, 1-3, 3-5, 5-10, 10+ |
| `Salary Hike Band` | `salary_hike_pct` | <8%, 8-10%, 10-12%, 12-15%, 15%+ |
| `Promotion Gap Band` | `years_since_promotion` | <1, 1-2, 2-3, 3-4, 4+ |
| `Age Band` | `age` | <25, 25-29, 30-34, 35-44, 45-54, 55+ |
| `AI Risk Band` | `perceived_ai_job_risk` | <3, 3-5, 5-7, 7+ |
